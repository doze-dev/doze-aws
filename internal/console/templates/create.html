The life of one message

Drawn from the numbers on the left. It redraws as you type.
  1. Sent
  2. A consumer receives itIt is hidden for , so nobody else gets it meanwhile.It is not hidden at all: with a visibility timeout of 0 another consumer can receive it at once.
  3. Not deleted in timeIt becomes visible again, and its receive count goes to 2.
  4. Received timesIts next receive moves it to instead of delivering it again.
  5. Never movedThere is no dead-letter queue, so it keeps coming back until it is deleted or expires.
  6. Never read at allIt is dropped after .
A received message that is deleted never reaches step 3. That is the whole point of deleting.

How a group behaves

  • Messages with the same group ID are delivered one after another, in the order sent.
  • While a consumer holds one, the next in that group waits. Other groups carry on.
  • So one stuck message holds back only its own group. A dead-letter queue lets it out of the way.
