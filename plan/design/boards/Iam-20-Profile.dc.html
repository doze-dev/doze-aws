What a profile is here
1A name that carries a role. On AWS an EC2 instance launched with the profile receives the role's permissions. There is no EC2 in doze-aws, so nothing launches with it: the profile is the IAM object a template or a provisioning tool creates, and the role it points at.
2One role, at most. A second is refused with LimitExceeded: Cannot exceed quota for InstanceSessionsPerInstanceProfile: 1.
3Deleting it. The confirm asks you to remove the role first. doze-aws does not insist; the profile goes and the role stays.
