The ruleSeventeen services, one pageEvery resource page has these eight parts, in this order, and nothing else at the top level.
1Rail. Full names on the wire, marks only inside a service. The room goes to the work.
2List. Every resource of this kind, with the one number that matters. A number that needs a look is coloured.
3Name and one action. The thing you most often do is the only solid button. Delete lives under ···, never beside it.
4Facts as a sentence. One line, read left to right. No strip of labelled boxes.
5Neighbours. What feeds it and what reads it, each a link.
6Tabs. The first is always what is inside it. Five at most.
7Try it. A composer at the foot of the content: do the thing by hand, with the same fields the API has.
8The wire, underneath. The calls that touched this resource, live. You never leave the page to see what your code did to it.
Selecting anything opens it on the right. A message, an item, an object, a call. It replaces this panel, in place. Nothing opens at the foot of the page or in a drawer over the content.
