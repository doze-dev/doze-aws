Kinesis

Five things on a stream ask first

From the stream's header · Delete
Delete stream “harbour-stock-events”? Its records go with it: the 9 records, the shards and the 1 registered consumer are removed from this machine. Anything putting to it afterwards gets ResourceNotFoundException.
Keep itDelete stream
After it: Kinesis opens on the next stream, with a toast. With none left, the empty state.
From a shard row · Split
Split shardId-000000000001 in two? The parent closes and two children open. Each takes half of its key range; the parent keeps its 5 records and stays readable.
Keep itSplit
After it: the Shards tab, with the closed parent above its two children.
From Details · Merge
Merge these two shards? Both close and one child opens covering their combined range: shardId-000000000000 and shardId-000000000001 become one shard holding 100.0% of the key space.
Keep themMerge
After it: the Shards tab. The parents stay readable until retention reclaims them.
From Details · Shard count
Rescale the stream? Existing shards close and a new set opens, tiling the key space evenly. 2 open shards become 4; records already written stay in the shards that close.
Keep itRescale
After it: the Shards tab, with a toast reading “Scaled to 4 shards”.
From Details · a consumer's row
Deregister “analytics-worker”? The registration is removed. Nothing was delivered over it: enhanced fan-out is not served here.
Keep itDeregister
After it: Details, with the consumer gone from the list.
What does not ask Put record, applying retention, setting the mode, encryption and shard-level metrics, registering a consumer, saving or emptying the policy, and saving tags. Each is the thing you came to do. Lowering retention is the one to watch: records past the new age are reclaimed by the next sweep.
