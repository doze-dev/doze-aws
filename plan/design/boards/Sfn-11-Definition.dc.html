The life of one waiting tasknew
Drawn from Reserve stock and the machine's own numbers.
ScheduledThe message goes to the queue with a token in it. The task waits for SendTaskSuccess or SendTaskFailure.
Silent for 60 sIt fails with States.HeartbeatTimeout. A SendTaskHeartbeat starts the 60 s over.
No answer in 300 sIt fails with States.Timeout, which the Retry matches: 2 more attempts, after 5 s and then 10 s, each with a fresh 300 s.
Any other failureNo Retry matches it. States.ALL catches it and Rejected receives it under $.error.
Three attempts of 300 s and 15 s of waiting is 915 s, inside the machine's 3600 s.
What is checked here The machine's TimeoutSeconds must be positive. A Task's TimeoutSeconds and HeartbeatSeconds only have to be numbers: 0, a negative, or a heartbeat longer than the timeout all pass. In doze-aws a Task timeout of 0 or less means no deadline, and a heartbeat only counts for a task that waits on a token.
